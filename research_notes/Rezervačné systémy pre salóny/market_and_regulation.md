## KQ1. How big is the addressable market in CZ and SK (number of businesses × realistic price) and how fast is booking-software adoption growing?

### Takeaway
The Czech register of economic subjects lists roughly 126–127 thousand entities in the "hairdressing & cosmetics / personal services" NACE-96 family (a gross count that includes dormant trade licences and probably the whole of NACE 96), while Slovakia's official sector counts could not be retrieved and Slovak self-employment is currently shrinking sharply (66k trade licences terminated vs 42k created in 2025). No survey on booking-software penetration in CZ/SK salons was found; the best proxy is global platform growth (Fresha ~60 % YoY run-rate growth, 130k businesses).

### Cited Findings
- **CZ business count (gross):** ceskadata.cz, drawing on the ČSÚ Register of Economic Subjects (RES), lists 126,657 firms (title of the same page says 126,389 — different snapshot dates) in the field "Kadeřnictví a kosmetika", classified by main activity under CZ-NACE 96; the count mixes natural persons (OSVČ) and legal persons — [ceskadata.cz](https://ceskadata.cz/obor/osobni-sluzby); ČSÚ RES description and open data — [ČSÚ RES](https://csu.gov.cz/registr_ekonomickych_subjektu), [Statistiky z RES](https://csu.gov.cz/statistiky-z-registru-ekonomickych-subjektu), [RES open data](https://csu.gov.cz/produkty/registr-ekonomickych-subjektu-otevrena-data).
- **CZ-NACE definition of 96.02** (hair washing/cutting/styling/colouring, shaving and beard care, facial massage, manicure/pedicure, make-up) — [CZ-NACE 96.02](http://www.nace.cz/962-kadernicke-kosmeticke-cinnosti-cinnosti-dennich-lazni-p); ČSÚ classification entry 96020 — [ČSÚ iSMS](https://apl2.czso.cz/iSMS/klaspol.jsp?kodklas=80004&kodcis=5107&ciselid=297038).
- **SK-NACE 96.02 "Kadernícke a kozmetické služby"** definition (same scope) — [nace.sk](http://www.nace.sk/nace/96-02-kadernicke-a-kozmeticke-sluzby/).
- **SK statistical coverage gap:** an information release of the Štatistický úrad SR on turnover in selected industries (September 2025) indicates the office no longer publishes turnover data for the "other activities" category (SK NACE 95 and 96), which contains hairdressing and cosmetics — [ŠÚ SR, Tržby za vlastné výkony a tovar, september 2025](https://slovak.statistics.sk/wps/portal/ext/products/informationmessages/inf_sprava_detail/b2bdd419-ba4d-4c7c-9004-caf05e166762/) (as summarised in search results; not verified on page).
- **SK self-employment dynamics 2025:** more than 66,000 trade licences (živnosti) were terminated in 2025 versus just over 42,000 created (net loss >24,000), attributed by FinStat mainly to higher social-contribution and consolidation measures; services (which include hairdressing, cosmetics, massage) were among the sectors with most new licences — [FinStat, Analýza vzniku a zániku firiem 2025](https://finstat.sk/nase-clanky/analyzy/Analyza-vzniku-a-zaniku-firiem-v-roku-2025); [TECHBYTE.sk](https://www.techbyte.sk/2026/02/zanik-zivnosti-slovensko-2025/); [peniaze.sk](https://www.peniaze.sk/zivnost/15456-rekordny-pocat-ukoncenych-zivnosti). 2024 also saw more terminations than creations, "from cafés and hairdressers to tradesmen" — [Startitup.sk](https://www.startitup.sk/zivnosti-na-slovensku-zanikaju-alarmujucim-tempom-podnikatelia-doplacaju-na-vysoke-odvody-a-dane/).
- **SK directory proxy (not a statistic):** Azet.sk catalogue lists ~900–2,000 hairdressing entries for Slovakia depending on view — [Azet katalóg kaderníctva](https://www.azet.sk/katalog/kadernictva/3/abc/slovensko/).
- **EU sector turnover:** total enterprise turnover of NACE 96.02 "hairdressing and other beauty treatment" across EU-27 was ≈ EUR 38,665 million in 2023 (Eurostat SBS as re-reported by Claight); structure "predominantly fragmented, anchored by micro-enterprises and self-employed" — [Claight hub (citing Eurostat)](https://hub.claight.com/statistics/hair-salons-in-eu); Eurostat SBS overview — [Eurostat SBS](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/web/structural-business-statistics).
- **Global platform adoption growth (proxy for adoption momentum):** Fresha reportedly serves 130,000 businesses processing 35 million appointments/month and USD 1.4 bn monthly transaction volume; ARR doubled in 2024 to USD 43.4 m and run-rate has since passed USD 140 m, growing ~60 % YoY — [Tracxn Fresha profile](https://tracxn.com/d/companies/fresha/__RtZ3_FAiFOVypRalj38jh-J45D_BbHAjdaAklWTJqko); [TAMradar](https://www.tamradar.com/funding-rounds/fresha-growth-80m); [BeautyMatter](https://beautymatter.com/articles/unwrapping-the-money-behind-beautys-booking-platforms).
- **Local CZ/SK vendors present (for pricing benchmarking, prices not captured):** Bookio (CZ/SK), Noona (CZ localisation), Reservio (CZ origin, "free" tier for hair salons), Salona, myFox — [Bookio kadeřnictví](https://www.bookio.com/cs/segmenty/kadernictvi); [Noona CZ](https://noona.app/hq/cs/rezervacni-system-pro-kadernictvi); [Reservio hair salon software](https://www.reservio.com/hair-salon-software); [Salona](https://salona.cz/business/rezervacni-system-pro-nehtova-studia); [myFox Top 5 rezervačních systémů ČR 2025](https://www.myfox.one/cs/top-5-rezervacnich-systemu-cr-2025/); [Salona blog 2026](https://salona.app/cs-cz/blog/novinky/nejlepsi-rezervacni-system-2026).
- **Reference price point (international):** Fresha, as of July 2026, charges USD 19.95/month (Independent) or USD 14.95/month per bookable team member (Team), plus card processing and a 20 % one-time commission on new marketplace clients; it retired the free-for-salons model in 2025 — [Twizzlo Fresha vs Booksy](https://twizzlo.com/articles/fresha-vs-booksy/); [Pabau](https://pabau.com/blog/fresha-vs-booksy/); [GoodCall](https://www.goodcall.com/appointment-scheduling-software/fresha-vs-booksy).

### Inferences
- The 126–127k Czech figure is an upper bound: (a) the page is titled "osobní služby" and describes CZ-NACE 96, so it very likely covers all of division 96 (incl. laundries, funeral services, physical well-being 96.04), not only 96.02; (b) RES contains many inactive/dormant trade licences. A realistic *active* 96.02 + 96.04 population is materially lower — the writer should pull ČSÚ RES open data filtered by NACE 96020/96040 and activity flag, or Eurostat SBS `sbs_sc_ovw` (ENT_NR for S9602/S9604, CZ/SK) — neither could be fetched here.
- Illustrative TAM arithmetic (assumptions, not findings): if ~40–60k active CZ establishments/practitioners in 96.02+96.04 and a realistic willingness-to-pay of CZK 300–700/month for an entry plan (Fresha's USD 15–20 equals roughly CZK 350–470), the CZ software-subscription TAM is on the order of CZK 150–500 m/year, before payments/SMS revenue. Slovakia is typically ~40–50 % of Czech volumes for SMB services but no sector count was retrievable; treat SK as a separate, smaller, currently contracting base of self-employed.
- Adoption growth in CZ/SK cannot be quantified from found sources; the presence of at least five local competitors (Bookio, Noona, Reservio, Salona, myFox) plus Fresha/Booksy indicates a contested but still-forming market.

## KQ3. What benchmarks exist for SMB vertical SaaS (churn, ARPU, payments share, CAC/LTV; Toast, Mindbody, Squire, Boulevard, Fresha)?

### Takeaway
Reference points: SMB SaaS logo churn of ~2–4 %/month is "healthy", vertical SaaS retains 30–50 % better than horizontal at the same ACV, and embedded payments typically exceed subscription revenue at scale (Mindbody ~USD 100 payments on ~USD 150 software per customer/month; Toast payments ≈ 85 % of revenue). Beauty-specific platforms monetise via low subscriptions + processing + marketplace commissions (Fresha 20 % on new clients).

### Cited Findings
- **SMB churn:** healthy average *monthly logo churn* in 2026 ≈ <0.5 % enterprise, 0.5–1.5 % mid-market, 2–4 % SMB/prosumer — [ChurnCost B2B SaaS churn benchmarks 2026](https://churncost.com/b2b-saas-churn-benchmarks-2026); related: [Livmo](https://livmo.com/blog/saas-churn-benchmarks-valuation/), [VantaInsights](https://vantainsights.com/insights/saas-churn-rate), [Ivris](https://ivristech.com/saas-churn-rate/).
- **Vertical vs horizontal retention:** vertical SaaS "systematically outperforms horizontal SaaS on gross retention, typically by 30 to 50 percent in the same ACV band", attributed to switching costs when the tool handles billing/compliance/operations — [Optifai (939-company dataset)](https://optif.ai/learn/questions/b2b-saas-churn-rate-benchmark/).
- **Tidemark 2025 Vertical & SMB SaaS Benchmark:** fintech-led companies show the strongest retention profile, followed by back-office; 59 % of surveyed companies offer more than one product, 41 % are single-product — [Tidemark 2025 report](https://www.tidemarkcap.com/vskp-chapter/2025-vertical-smb-saas-benchmark-report); Stripe also publishes a 2025 vertical SaaS benchmark (not fetched) — [Stripe vertical SaaS benchmark 2025](https://stripe.com/lp/vertical-saas-benchmark-2025); metrics framing — [Mostly Metrics](https://www.mostlymetrics.com/p/the-top-metrics-for-vertical-saas).
- **Mindbody unit economics:** earned ≈ USD 250/customer/month: ≈ USD 150/month (~USD 1,800/yr) software plus ≈ USD 100/month payments; embedded payments >50 % of revenue; for the three years before IPO payments were >⅓ of revenue; Mindbody "makes more off processing gym payments than off subscriptions" — [a16z, Fintech Scales Vertical SaaS](https://a16z.com/fintech-scales-vertical-saas/); [Gusto Embedded blog](https://embedded.gusto.com/blog/embedded-fintech-strategy-revenue-churn/); [Rainforest Pay](https://www.rainforestpay.com/blog/why-vertical-saas-platforms-cant-afford-to-ignore-embedded-payments).
- **Toast:** FY2024 subscription revenue USD 706 m (~70 % margin) vs payments revenue USD 4.1 bn (~22 % margin) → payments ≈ 85 %+ of revenue; Q2 2025 "Financial Technology Solutions" revenue +25 % YoY — [Fractal Software, Vertical SaaS Fintech Playbook](https://www.fractalsoftware.com/perspectives/the-vertical-saas-fintech-playbook); [Apideck](https://www.apideck.com/blog/embedded-finance-vertical-saas).
- **ISV share of acquiring:** ISVs accounted for 29 % of merchant-acquiring revenue in 2022, expected 37 % by 2025; Bain projects US embedded-finance transaction value > USD 7 tn by end-2026 (from USD 2.6 tn in 2021) — [SaaS Mag](https://www.saasmag.com/embedded-finance-saas-payments-lending-banking/); [Kanopy](https://kanopylabs.com/blog/how-to-build-embedded-finance-for-saas).
- **Category valuations:** Squire went private; Mindbody was acquired by Vista for USD 1.9 bn; Boulevard raised USD 70 m at a USD 1 bn+ valuation — [SMB Vertical SaaS Fundraising Guide 2026](https://startupfundraising.com/smb-vertical-saas-fundraising).
- **Fresha economics:** USD 285 m total funding, USD 1 bn+ valuation after an USD 80 m primary round led by KKR (May 2026); ARR USD 43.4 m end-2024 (doubled YoY), run-rate > USD 140 m (+~60 % YoY); model = low entry subscription + marketplace commission on new clients — [TAMradar](https://www.tamradar.com/funding-rounds/fresha-growth-80m); [Tracxn](https://tracxn.com/d/companies/fresha/__RtZ3_FAiFOVypRalj38jh-J45D_BbHAjdaAklWTJqko); [GoodCall](https://www.goodcall.com/appointment-scheduling-software/fresha-vs-booksy); [CB Insights Booksy vs Fresha](https://www.cbinsights.com/compare/booksy-1-vs-shedul).
- **PLG / freemium conversion:** SMB-focused B2B SaaS typically converts 2–5 % free→paid; Databox survey of SMB-serving SaaS: 3–10 %; broad B2B freemium 1–5 %, tightly targeted high-intent apps 5–15 %; top-quartile 30-day freemium→paid 8–15 %; a 2026 meta-analysis of 190 PLG companies found paid-traffic freemium conversion of 2.8 % lifted to 4.9 % with activation-first landing pages; overall average free-to-paid ≈ 9 %, freemium visitor conversion median 12 % — [ProductLed benchmarks](https://productled.com/blog/product-led-growth-benchmarks); [daydream](https://www.withdaydream.com/library/insights/freemium-conversion-rate); [Guru Startups](https://www.gurustartups.com/reports/freemium-to-paid-conversion-rate-benchmarks); [Pulseahead](https://www.pulseahead.com/blog/trial-to-paid-conversion-benchmarks-in-saas); [ADV.me](https://adv.me/articles/conversion-optimization/saas-free-trial-conversion-rate-benchmarks-2025/).

### Inferences
- For a CZ/SK salon SaaS, plan on 2–3 %/month logo churn (≈ 25–30 %/yr) for the smallest self-employed tier and materially better retention once payments/POS/eKasa receipts are embedded (Tidemark's "fintech-led best retention" finding).
- Fresha's run-rate of ~USD 140 m on 130k businesses implies ≈ USD 90/business/month blended (subscription + processing + marketplace) — roughly 4–6x its list subscription price, illustrating why payments and marketplace fees, not subscriptions, drive revenue in this vertical. (Derived; the two figures may come from different dates.)
- CAC/LTV: no vertical-specific CAC figures were found; with ARPU of CZK 400–700/month and 2.5 % monthly churn, LTV ≈ CZK 16–28k, so a sustainable blended CAC is well under CZK 10k — consistent with PLG/freemium and marketplace-led acquisition rather than outbound sales.

- No primary filings retrieved (Mindbody 10-K, Toast 10-K, Lightspeed); all figures are second-hand.
- No published CAC, payback or NRR figures specific to salon/beauty SaaS (Boulevard, Squire, Booksy, Vagaro, Zenoti, GlossGenius) were found; BeautyMatter's article likely has more but was blocked.

## KQ4. Technology and business trends 2024–2026: AI receptionists, WhatsApp Business API, Reserve with Google, Instagram/Facebook and Apple booking, embedded payments, marketplace vs subscription

### Takeaway
2025–26 saw (a) incumbents ship AI phone receptionists (Fresha AI Concierge, ~A$230/location/month) while a wave of third-party AI-receptionist tools integrates into Fresha/Vagaro/Boulevard; (b) Meta move WhatsApp to per-message pricing (1 July 2025) with utility messages ~80–95 % cheaper than marketing; (c) Reserve with Google remain partner-gated with strict eligibility (single location, matching GBP data, online payment method); (d) the leading marketplace (Fresha) abandon "free" in favour of subscription + 20 % new-client commission.

### Cited Findings
- **Fresha AI Concierge:** AI phone receptionist built into Fresha that answers inbound calls 24/7, books/reschedules/cancels directly into the live calendar; "rolled out properly in 2026"; priced at A$229.95 per location per month (AU pricing) — [Fresha AI Concierge](https://www.fresha.com/for-business/features/ai-concierge); [Booked By Gordon](https://bookedbygordon.com/blog/is-there-a-voice-agent-for-fresha).

### Inferences

## KQ5. Which legal obligations concretely shape product design for a CZ/SK/EU booking SaaS?

### Takeaway
Concrete product constraints found: (1) e-mail/SMS marketing needs opt-in unless the recipient is an existing customer buying similar services (CZ zákon 480/2004 §7; SK zákon 452/2021 §116 — tightened by a November 2025 amendment, existing-customer exception limited in time); (2) health/contraindication notes for massage or cosmetics are Art. 9 GDPR special-category data requiring explicit consent; (3) online bookings are distance contracts — the 14-day withdrawal right applies unless a §1837 exception fits, and the "leisure service on a specific date" exception is the one usually relied on; (4) card-on-file for deposits/no-show fees must be set up with SCA and charged as merchant-initiated transactions; (5) CZ has no fiscal receipt system after EET but must issue a receipt on request (§16 z. 634/1992); SK hairdressers/cosmeticians accepting cash or card must use eKasa and, per the 2025 amendment, must accept cashless payments from 1 May 2026 (dates reported inconsistently); (6) the EAA/z. 424/2023 (effective 28 June 2025) covers B2C e-commerce services but exempts micro-enterprise service providers.

### Cited Findings

**Marketing consent (ePrivacy transposition)**
- **CZ zákon č. 480/2004 Sb.:** commercial communications may not be sent without the recipient's consent unless the recipient is the sender's *customer*; the law applies to all mass electronic contact including SMS; a "customer" is a person who has entered into a contractual relationship (paid or unpaid) with the trader; supervision lies with ÚOOÚ — [ÚOOÚ FAQ k zákonu 480/2004](https://uoou.gov.cz/cinnost/obchodni-sdeleni/casto-kladene-otazky-k-zakonu-c-4802004-sb); [ÚOOÚ Obchodní sdělení Q&A](https://uoou.gov.cz/index.php/profesional/qa-otazky-a-odpovedi/obchodni-sdeleni); [epravo.cz](https://www.epravo.cz/top/clanky/casto-kladene-otazky-k-zakonu-c-4802004-sb-o-nekterych-sluzbach-informacni-spolecnosti-28646.html); [DAUC](https://www.dauc.cz/clanky/6181/zasilani-obchodnich-sdeleni-v-souladu-se-zakonem-o-nekterych-sluzbach-informacni-spolecnosti); labelling requirements — [HSP & Partners](https://www.akhsp.cz/novinky/jak-spravne-oznacit-obchodni-sdeleni); evidencing consent — [Havel & Partners](https://www.havelpartners.blog/mate-souhlas-adresata-k-zasilani-obchodnich-sdeleni-a-mohl-by-ho-soud-videt); MPO overview — [MPO](https://mpo.gov.cz/cz/e-komunikace-a-posta/postovni-sluzby/sluzby-informacni-spolecnosti/zakon-c--480-2004-sb---o-nekterych-sluzbach-informacni-spolecnosti--84535/).
- **SK zákon č. 452/2021 Z. z. o elektronických komunikáciách (§116 nevyžiadaná komunikácia), effective 1 Feb 2022:** direct marketing by e-mail/SMS requires demonstrable consent unless the existing-customer exception applies (customer already bought a product/service; marketing concerns *similar* products/services); after one year from the end of the contractual relationship the contact must be moved to a consent-based regime — [Podnikajte.sk](https://www.podnikajte.sk/zakonne-povinnosti-podnikatela/priamy-marketing-pravna-uprava-od-1-2-2022); [Osobnyudaj.sk FAQ](https://www.osobnyudaj.sk/faq/kedy-je-potrebny-suhlas-zakaznika-na-zasielanie-marketingovych-e-mailov/); [GDPR Academy](https://gdpracademy.sk/docs/priamy-marketing-po-novele-zakona-o-elektronickych-komunikaciach-prepojenie-s-pravidlami-gdpr/); [Bird & Bird SK direct marketing note (2022)](https://www.twobirds.com/-/media/new-website-content/insights/pdfs/direct-marketing_en_apr2022.pdf). Supervisory authority: Úrad pre reguláciu elektronických komunikácií a poštových služieb — [teleoff.gov.sk](https://www.teleoff.gov.sk/urad/odbory-oddelenia/odbor-statneho-dohladu-elektronickych-komunikacii/nevyziadana-komunikacia/informacie-verejnost-ohladom-nevyziadanej-komunikacie/).
- **SK amendment effective 12 November 2025** "fundamentally changes §116 and tightens telemarketing, e-mail and SMS marketing" — [Legalion, Priamy marketing (30 Dec 2025)](https://www.legalion.sk/2025/12/30/priamy-marketing-ako-oslovovat-klientov-legalne/); background on telemarketing changes — [epravo.sk](https://www.epravo.sk/top/clanky/nove-pravidla-tele-marketingu-uvolnovanie-pravidiel-v-neprospech-volaneho-5324.html); [Avris](https://avris.sk/priamy-marketing-po-novele-zakona/). Exact new wording not retrieved.

**GDPR — health data in salons**
- Art. 9 GDPR prohibits processing of special-category data including health data unless an exception applies, e.g. explicit consent (Art. 9(2)(a)) — [gdpr-info.eu Art. 9](https://gdpr-info.eu/art-9-gdpr/); ÚOOÚ's basic guide to data protection — [ÚOOÚ Základní příručka](https://uoou.gov.cz/verejnost/zakladni-prirucka-k-ochrane-udaju).
- Practice: Czech massage studios require clients to disclose health conditions affecting the massage, list contraindications, and recommend a signed informed-consent form covering health risks/contraindications — [Beyond Massage GDPR & podmínky](https://beyondmassage.cz/podminky/); [KN Masáže GDPR](https://www.knmasaze.com/gdpr); [Terapeutické masáže GDPR](https://terapeuticke-masaze.cz/gdpr); public-health office model operating rules for massage — [KHS Pardubice provozní řád – masáže (vzor)](https://www.khspce.cz/wp-content/uploads/2020/10/provozni-rad-masaze.doc).

**Consumer law — withdrawal, specific-date services, deposits**
- CZ withdrawal from distance service contracts is governed by §1837 and §1840 of zákon č. 89/2012 Sb. (občanský zákoník); a consumer *cannot* withdraw where accommodation, transport, catering or *leisure-related services* are contracted for a specific date (§1837 písm. j)); where part of a service was already provided with the consumer's express consent, the consumer pays a proportionate part; otherwise the trader must refund all money received — [ČOI FAQ 7: Odstoupení do 14 dnů u služeb](https://coi.gov.cz/faq/7-odstoupeni-od-smlouvy-do-14-dnu-u-sluzeb/); [ČOI FAQ b) V jakých případech nemohu odstoupit](https://coi.gov.cz/faq/b-v-jakych-pripadech-nemohu-od-smlouvy-odstoupit-6/); [ČOI FAQ 5](https://coi.gov.cz/faq/5-odstoupeni-od-smlouvy-do-14-dnu-u-sluzeb-2/); [ČOI FAQ 8](https://coi.gov.cz/faq/8-odstoupeni-od-smlouvy-do-14-dnu-u-sluzeb/); consumer-association explainer — [SOS Asociace: Distanční smlouvy – co nelze vrátit](https://www.asociace-sos.cz/distancni-smlouvy-co-nelze-vratit-do-14-dnu-a-proc/); [Dostupný advokát](https://dostupnyadvokat.cz/blog/odstoupeni-od-smlouvy).

**Payments — PSD2 / SCA and card-on-file**
- Merchant-initiated transactions (delayed charges, usage-based billing, **no-show fees**) are out of SCA scope; the card must be authenticated (SCA) when saved for later use and subsequent charges flagged as MIT; series of equal recurring payments are exempt after an authenticated initial set-up (credential-on-file) — [Stripe SCA guide](https://stripe.com/guides/strong-customer-authentication); [Recurly on MITs](https://blog.recurly.com/dunning-for-the-win-merchant-initiated-transactions-and-psd2); [Merchant Fraud Journal](https://www.merchantfraudjournal.com/psd2-sca-requirements/); booking-context explanation (deposits, cancellation and no-show fees via MIT with proof of SCA) — [SiteMinder](https://www.siteminder.com/strong-customer-authentication/); [NMI](https://support.nmi.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360016094797-PSD2-Strong-Customer-Authentication-SCA-What-It-Means-to-You).

**Receipts / fiscalisation**
- **CZ:** after the end of EET, the duty to issue a receipt *on the consumer's request* remains under §16 zákona č. 634/1992 Sb. (o ochraně spotřebitele): date of sale/service, description of the service, price, and seller identification (name/firm, IČO); the former EET receipt never replaced this document — [Podnikatel.cz: EET skončila – jak je to s účtenkami](https://www.podnikatel.cz/clanky/eet-skoncila-jak-je-to-ale-nyni-s-uctenkami-a-dalsimi-doklady/); [ČOI FAQ d) doklad o poskytnutí služby](https://coi.gov.cz/faq/d-musi-mi-podnikatel-vystavit-doklad-o-poskytnuti-sluzby/); [ČOI FAQ 2 kupní doklad](https://coi.gov.cz/faq/2-je-prodavajici-povinen-mi-vystavit-kupni-doklad/); [§16 text](http://zakony.centrum.cz/zakon-o-ochrane-spotrebitele/cast-2-paragraf-16); [jiranek.cz](https://www.jiranek.cz/inpage/kdy-a-jaky-doklad-je-prodavajici-povinen-vystavit-zakaznikovi/).
- **SK eKasa:** hairdressing services must register revenue in eKasa if they accept cash *or card* at the point of sale; cosmetics services, previously outside, must now register as well — [Účtovníctvo pre každého](https://uctovnictvoprekazdeho.sk/ako-podnikat-ako-kadernicka-kozmeticka-zivnost-priestory-ekasa/); [ProÚčtovník](https://prouctovnik.sk/uctovnik-pre-kadernictvo-beauty/); [eKasaExpert – eKasa 2026, novela 384/2025](https://ekasaexpert.sk/blog/ekasa-2026-zmeny.html).
- **SK mandatory cashless acceptance:** the law took effect 1 January 2026 with the obligation to accept cashless payments (card, mobile, or QR) from **1 May 2026** for transactions above EUR 1, removing previous EUR 5/10 minimums; a cash-only hairdresser using eKasa must therefore also offer a cashless option — [Finsider](https://www.finsider.sk/ekonomika/podnikatelia-musia-zacat-prijimat-platby-kartou-odmietnut-ich-nebudu-moct-ani-pri-malych-sumach/); [FinReport / Grant Thornton](https://www.grantthornton.sk/novinky/system-ekasa-je-povinny-nielen-pre-podnikatelov-ale-aj-pri-predaji-z-dvora-od-maja-musi-kazdy-prijimat-aj-bezhotovostne-platby); [Papaya POS](https://papayapos.sk/povinnost-platby-kartou/). **Conflict:** an earlier Podnikajte.sk article (from the legislative-proposal stage) reported the obligation as starting 1 July 2025 — [Podnikajte.sk](https://www.podnikajte.sk/pripravovane-zmeny-v-legislative/bezhotovostne-platby-ako-povinnost-od-1-7-2025); the 2026-dated sources should be preferred.

**Accessibility (EAA)**
- EAA Art. 4(5) exempts *micro-enterprises providing services* (<10 persons and ≤ EUR 2 m turnover or balance sheet); exemption applies to services only, lapses immediately once thresholds are exceeded; e-commerce services must be accessible from 28 June 2025 — [Taylor Wessing](https://www.taylorwessing.com/en/interface/2025/accessibility/key-eu-accessibility-act-exemptions-and-the-challenges-they-pose); [Accessible.org e-commerce requirements](https://accessible.org/eaa-ecommerce-services-requirements/); [Level Access](https://www.levelaccess.com/compliance-overview/european-accessibility-act-eaa/); [Kris Rivenburgh on micro exemption](https://krisrivenburgh.com/microenterprises-exempt-eaa-requirements/).
- **CZ transposition — zákon č. 424/2023 Sb.** effective 28 June 2025; applies to B2C e-commerce (online sale of goods *and services* to consumers) regardless of platform; micro-enterprise service providers exempt; pure B2B de facto excluded; ČOI is the enforcement body for services — [zákon 424/2023 Sb.](https://www.zakonyprolidi.cz/cs/2023-424); [ČOI – přístupnost pro podnikatele](https://coi.gov.cz/pro-podnikatele/pristupnost-vyrobku-a-sluzeb-pro-podnikatele/); [eLegal](https://elegal.cz/e-shopy-a-nova-pravidla-pristupnosti-co-byste-meli-vedet-); [Shop5 FAQ](https://www.shop5.cz/clanek/faq-pristupnost-e-shopu-a-zakon-c-424-2023-sb-na-koho-se-skutecne-vztahuje/); [DataStorm](https://www.datastorm.cz/blog/pristupnost-webu-povinnosti-2026); [AK Cisek](https://www.akcisek.cz/blog/novy-zakon-o-pristupnosti-vyrobku-a-sluzeb-co-prinasi-a-koho-se-tyka).

### Inferences (product-design implications; plus background framework not verified in this session)
- **Consent flows:** separate (a) transactional messages (confirmations, reminders — not commercial communications) from (b) marketing (promo SMS/e-mail/WhatsApp marketing templates). Default marketing to *opt-in* checkbox unless the salon confirms the client is an existing customer and the message concerns similar services; store consent evidence (timestamp, IP, wording); every marketing message must be labelled as commercial, identify the sender and carry a free opt-out (CZ §7 480/2004; SK §116). For SK, implement an automatic expiry: 12 months after the last purchase the client drops out of the "existing customer" exception. Post-Nov-2025 SK wording should be re-checked.
- **Health notes:** treat "contraindications / allergies / pregnancy / skin conditions" fields as Art. 9 data: explicit, granular, revocable consent captured in the booking or intake form; restrict access by role; define retention (e.g., delete X years after last visit) and include in the DPA. Salon = controller, SaaS = processor → Art. 28 DPA with sub-processor list (SMS/WhatsApp gateway, payment provider, hosting); EU data residency preferred. (Art. 28 framework from background knowledge — [GDPR Art. 28](https://gdpr-info.eu/art-28-gdpr/) not fetched.)
- **Cancellation/deposit terms:** since an online booking is a distance contract, product T&Cs should either (i) frame the appointment as a service on a specific date and rely on §1837 j) (strong for wellness/leisure, weaker for a haircut, which may not be "využití volného času"), and/or (ii) obtain the consumer's *express request* to begin performance before the 14-day period ends with acknowledgement of losing the withdrawal right (§1837 a)/§1834 mechanics). Storno/no-show fees must be disclosed pre-contractually and be proportionate; ČOI/dTest guidance on storno fee proportionality was not retrieved.
- **Card-on-file:** save cards via an SCA-authenticated set-up (SetupIntent-type flow), disclose no-show policy at booking, and charge later as MIT; do not store PANs (PCI scope via tokenising gateway).
- **Receipts:** CZ — generate a §16-compliant receipt/simplified tax document on request (no fiscal signature needed after EET); SK — either integrate a certified eKasa/ORP solution (virtual or online cash register) or explicitly leave fiscalisation to the salon's POS; note that card payments in SK also trigger the eKasa duty, which matters for embedded payments.
- **Accessibility:** most salons are micro-enterprises and exempt, but the SaaS vendor's own hosted booking pages are a B2C e-commerce service the vendor provides; once the vendor exceeds 10 staff/EUR 2 m, WCAG-level accessibility of the widget becomes a legal requirement (EAA/z. 424/2023, supervised by ČOI). Building accessible by default is the low-cost path.
- **Background framework not verified in this session (writer should check EUR-Lex):** VAT on B2C SaaS follows the customer's Member State above the EUR 10,000 EU-wide micro-threshold via OSS (Directive 2006/112/EC as amended, [EUR-Lex 32006L0112](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2006/112/oj)); B2B cross-border SaaS is reverse-charged. AI Act (Reg. 2024/1689, [EUR-Lex 32024R1689](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1689/oj)) Art. 50 transparency: people interacting with an AI system (chat/voice receptionist) must be informed they are dealing with AI, applicable from 2 August 2026; a booking bot is not high-risk. DSA (Reg. 2022/2065, [EUR-Lex 32022R2065](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2022/2065/oj)): a consumer-facing marketplace listing salons is an "online platform" but micro/small enterprises are exempt from Section 3 platform duties (Art. 19), while basic hosting duties (notice-and-action, contact points, T&C transparency) still apply. NIS2 (Dir. 2022/2555, [EUR-Lex 32022L2555](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2022/2555/oj)) covers cloud/SaaS providers generally only from medium size (≥50 staff or ≥ EUR 10 m turnover); a small booking SaaS is out of scope unless designated. Consumer Rights Directive 2011/83/EU Art. 16(l) is the EU source of the CZ §1837 j) exception ([EUR-Lex 32011L0083](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2011/83/oj)). Czech EET was abolished by zákon č. 458/2022 Sb. effective 1 January 2023 (date from background knowledge).

### Gaps

- ČOI/dTest guidance on the lawfulness and proportionality of storno (cancellation) fees and non-refundable deposits for personal services: not retrieved.

## KQ6. Which local integrations are effectively mandatory in CZ/SK (payment gateways, accounting, SMS gateways and prices, QR payments, Apple/Google Pay)?

### Takeaway
Czech SMS costs CZK ~0.4–1.1 per message (GoSMS cheapest at ~CZK 0.41–0.76, BulkGate ~0.68–0.74, TopSMS ~0.88–0.98, SMSbrána ~0.89–1.08, plus monthly sender-ID fees); Comgate positions itself as the low-cost local gateway (Easy tariff 1 % + CZK 0 per transaction, ~CZK 100/month in its example). Accounting, QR-payment and wallet-adoption facts could not be retrieved.

### Cited Findings
- **SMS gateway prices (CZ, verified by comparison sites as of 2026):** GoSMS from CZK 0.41/SMS (final price depends on configuration and monthly fees; another comparison lists average CZK 0.76); BulkGate ≈ CZK 0.74 via system number (average CZK 0.677); TopSMS CZK 0.88–0.98 without monthly sender-ID fee; SMSbrána.cz CZK 0.89–1.08 depending on credit volume (average CZK 1.05); monthly fees for a custom sender name/ID must be added — [SMSVio: Kolik stojí SMS brána v ČR (2026)](https://www.smsvio.cz/cena-sms-brany/); [TopSMS srovnání](https://www.topsms.cz/srovnani); [SMSbrána ceník](https://www.smsbrana.cz/cenik); [GoSMS ceník](https://www.gosms.eu/cs/cenik/); [BulkGate](https://www.bulkgate.com/en/products/sms-gateway/); [Webtrh recenze SMS brány](https://webtrh.cz/recenze/sms-brany/).

### Inferences
- A reminder SMS in CZ costs roughly CZK 0.5–1.0; at 2 reminders per booking and ~150 bookings/month a solo practitioner's SMS cost is ~CZK 150–300/month — comparable to a whole entry-level subscription, which is why local vendors sell SMS credits separately and why WhatsApp/e-mail/push reminders are attractive margin levers.
- For SK, integrating with eKasa-certified POS/virtual cash-register providers (or partnering with one) becomes quasi-mandatory once the SaaS handles payment at the point of service, because card acceptance both triggers eKasa and is compulsory from May 2026.
- Payment-gateway choice: Comgate/GoPay give local methods (Czech bank buttons, QR) at ~1 % but Stripe/Adyen give MIT/card-on-file tooling and Reserve-with-Google-compatible flows; a vendor will likely need one local gateway for CZK/EUR bank transfers/QR and one global PSP for card-on-file. (Stripe CZ pricing was not verified.)
