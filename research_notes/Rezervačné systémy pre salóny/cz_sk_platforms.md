**Method note / limitation (important for the report writer):** The research environment's egress proxy blocked *every* direct page fetch (vendor pricing pages, CzechCrunch, Forbes.cz, Lupa.cz, finstat.sk, Google Play, Capterra, Wikipedia, etc.), and the session's web-search budget was exhausted after ~75 queries. ## Key Question 1: Which platforms exist, are active, and dominate the CZ/SK salon-beauty-wellness booking market; roughly how many businesses does each serve?

### Takeaway
No independent market-share data exists for CZ/SK. By self-reported business counts the largest players are Notino Partner (8,000–9,000 salons across CZ/SK/HU/RO/BG, free), Reservio (global, 130 countries, ~15M bookings/yr, Brno-based) and Bookio (2,500+ businesses, Bratislava-based); Reservanto (Liberec) is the most-cited freemium alternative in CZ. Global marketplaces are weak locally: Booksy operates in CZ only marginally (no official CZ launch found), Treatwell is not present in CZ/SK, Fresha lists only a few dozen SK venues. A long tail of small Czech (myFox, Salona, Noona HQ, SuperSaaS, Zaptime, Notado, Reenio, Termino, Reservatic, YourSalon, Altegio) and Slovak (EasyBooking, MojaRezervácia, Planivo, RezervacnyPortal.eu, Rezervujto, Fiona/HeadMaster, Visblee, Apponio) systems compete on price.

### Inferences
- The CZ market is unusually fragmented and price-driven, with a dominant *free* option (Notino Partner) that is a retailer's customer-acquisition channel rather than a SaaS business; this likely caps what pure SaaS vendors can charge (most sit at 166–500 Kč/month).
- Notino Partner's growth from 7,000 (Dec 2025) to 8,000+ (Jul 2026) salons across five countries suggests it is now the largest single salon-booking network in CZ/SK by venue count, though the CZ/SK split is unknown.
- Bookio's 2025 revenue (~€0.86M + €0.38M across two entities) and Reservio's 2019 target (~USD 1M) imply both are small SMEs, not venture-scale companies; ABUGO's <300M CZK turnover covers four companies.
- Booksy's CZ presence appears organic (Polish-border and expat barbers) rather than a supported market launch; Treatwell is absent, so the "marketplace" role in CZ/SK is effectively filled by Notino Partner and Reservio's directory.

## Key Question 2: Exact current pricing (plans, monthly fees, SMS fees, marketplace commission, free tiers, trial length)

### Takeaway
Typical CZ SaaS pricing is free tier + 166–800 Kč/month; SK vendors price €6.90–€35/month. Free tiers usually cap bookings (Reservio 40/30 days, myFox START 40, SuperSaaS 50) or calendars (Zaptime, Noona). SMS reminders are almost always extra (0.69–1.50 Kč per SMS or credit packs), except myFox (200 SMS included), Salona and Noona Premium (unlimited). Notino Partner is fully free with no commission on bookings.

### Takeaway
Complaint themes: (a) marketplaces' acquisition fees and attribution (Booksy Boost charging for clients who found the salon via Google; Treatwell's 35% first-booking fee), (b) support quality and unresponsiveness (Booksy; Reservio for free-tier users), (c) SMS reminders failing or costing extra (Reservio), (d) limited customization and staff-unfriendly UI (Reservio), (e) unauthorised or stale listings and reviews being suppressed by businesses (Booksy).
